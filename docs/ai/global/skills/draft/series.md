# Series episode rules

> Version: **series-en-v1** (2026-10-17) · added only to the design and script stages of a series episode (global spec 6.11, Korean spec/11). Regular episodes do not read it.

- This episode is one part of a multi-episode series. It is complete on its own. A listener who missed earlier episodes can follow it.
- Cover only this episode's scope in depth. Do not build a section on a concept another episode covers in depth. Name it in one sentence at most when needed.
- Do not explain again at length what earlier episodes covered. Recall it in one sentence for new listeners.
- From episode 2, the recap is one or two sentences at the start of the first explainer turn of the Opening. Say only the main point of the previous episode, from the given claim. The host does not state what the previous episode said.
- Use the given series intro line and the given sign-off sentence word for word. Do not change or extend them.
- Do not explain the next episode's content. The sign-off sentence is the only preview.
