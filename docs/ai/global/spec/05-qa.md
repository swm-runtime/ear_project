# spec/05 (global) — QA

> English spec chapter for the global pipeline (`PIPELINE_LOCALE=en`). Index and decisions: `docs/ai/global/PIPELINE.md` 6.5.

## 1. Purpose

QA checks that the script says only what the sources say. It does not judge style; the critic does. QA runs independently: it never sees the planning or writing context.

## 2. Inputs — three only

`sources.md` (excerpts) · `script.md` · `claims.md`. Excerpts may be Korean; claims and script are English.

## 3. Checks

| # | Check | What fails |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fact match | A statement does not match its excerpt. For Korean excerpts, the English restatement changes the meaning |
| 2 | Unsupported | A number, quote, name, cause, or conclusion is not in the excerpts |
| 3 | Distortion | A statement is stronger, broader, or more certain than its source |
| 4 | Omission | Missing context makes a statement misleading |
| 5 | Templates | The intro, closing lead, or sign-off frame is changed; the two `{episode_line}` fills differ; turns follow the sign-off |
| 6 | Notation and banned forms | Spelling rules for Korean words (spec/04 5), tables, URLs, relative time words (now for the present era, today, nowadays, these days, currently, recently, this year, last year, next year) outside the fixed template words, number and currency forms |
| 7 | Length | Checked by code (L0 floor in spoken words); QA records the approximate count and marks hold |
| 8 | Speaker — where facts sit | A factual statement in a host turn, including a host question that assumes a case, condition, or result not yet explained |
| 9 | Speaker — reaction strength | A host reaction makes a claim bigger or more certain |
| 10 | Speaker — roles | Labels or roles differ from the fixed roles: E turns are the explainer (the first name in `PIPELINE_SPEAKERS`), Y turns are the host, and each name keeps one letter |
| 11 | Real people and sensitive content | Private life, rumors, or unconfirmed matters of real people; stereotypes or rankings of cultures or groups |

- Checks 1 to 4 are the core. A factual statement with no claim behind it fails check 2.
- Only spoken lines are judged. Section headers are not read aloud.

## 4. Verdict and retries

QA pass → `qa_passed`. Fail → findings with location and reason go to a fix call → QA again. At most three QA runs; after three failures the episode goes to `review_required`. L0 fixes are counted separately (at most 3) and are never passed to QA as prior failures.

## 5. Done when

- Given a script with a statement not in the excerpts, When QA runs, Then it fails check 2 with the turn and the statement.
- Given a script that passes, When QA runs again on the unchanged script, Then the verdict is the same.
